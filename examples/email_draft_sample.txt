Subject: Signal Weekly — Top 3 issues, all pointing to the same root cause

Team,

Only one rep interview this cycle, but it's loaded with signal, and three issues stand out as blocking real work. Sharing the top 3 — full digest has 5 plus bugs and data flags if you want the rest.

1. GPO/wholesaler data isn't where reps look for it. This is the single most-mentioned issue (7 times). Reps are trained to check GPO/wholesaler first thing, every account, every call — and it's buried or missing. Quote: "everything always as a rule you need to have GO wholesaler up top... that's like our eye is trained to go there." Action: put a GPO/wholesaler block at the top of account and opportunity pages, and auto-populate pricing from the linked contract.

2. No way to flag lapsed or declining accounts. Reps are tracking reorder timing on sticky notes because we don't do it for them. Quote: "I got to keep track with 8,000 little sticky notes here." Action: build a reorder-due flag based on historical purchase cadence and auto-create the opportunity.

3. No real-time inventory visibility, and it's costing us credibility in the field. A rep closed a 2,000-unit deal and could only deliver 890 because there's no allocation data available before commitment. This one's escalating — he said he's "going to beat that drum every time we talk." Action: scope inventory data integration and get an availability indicator on the product card before Q3 planning.

One more thing worth flagging even though it didn't make the top 3: closing an opportunity currently makes its history disappear, and when a discontinued product gets relaunched a year later, we lose everything. That's a real risk if it's happening broadly, not just an edge case.

Let me know if you want to discuss any of these before Thursday's sprint.

Luke
